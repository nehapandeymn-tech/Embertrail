## Implemented gameplay

### Controls and jump feel

- Choose a starter and click Play. Space, Up, or a mouse click jumps; hold for more height.
- Esc pauses and releases focus. P toggles pause/resume; Enter starts/restarts.
- Input is local to the focused game. **Embertrail does not implement Dockosaur's global Right Option or Control-Option-Space controls and does not require Input Monitoring.** The user shared Dockosaur's installation message as inspiration and later as a format for sharing Embertrail.
- Jump feel was compared with `/Users/neha/dockosaur/Sources/main.swift`, but that separate project was not modified.
- Current gravity 650, tap launch velocity 220, held launch velocity 235: approximately 37 px / 0.68 s for a tap and 42 px / 0.72 s held.
- 160 ms jump buffer, 100 ms coyote time, no automatic jumps caused by OS key-repeat. A press during turbo cannot cut its automatic jump short.

### Crates and visible hazards

- Descending onto a crate top is safe. Press again, or buffer a press before landing, to jump off it. This is a jump renewed by landing, not an unrestricted extra midair jump.
- Side/underside crate hits do not end the run. They deduct up to 10 existing points once per crate, physically push the collision body backward, show the deduction, then recover forward. Score never goes negative or creates future score debt.
- Giant/star/shield powers can protect against crate hits. Crate landing height, jump origin, leaving a platform, pause, and restart are handled.
- **Crabs were removed entirely from spawning/game kinds.** Burrowing moles remain.
- Spikes are groups of three, with an 18×8 collision area. Ordinary rocks/boulders were enlarged. Hazard timing still allows at least 180 ms slack for a tap jump.
- Ordinary pattern gaps are 1.8–2.5 seconds early, then at least 1.25 seconds.

### Fruit, points, and evolution

Regular collectible balls/streams were replaced by single occasional fruit pickups. At most one uncollected fruit exists; spawns are at least 9 seconds apart, usually 9–14 seconds or longer during other encounters/evolution. The first varieties introduce heart, lightning, then giant; later varieties are random.

| Fruit | Points | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Heart | 75 | Large score bonus |
| Dark blue | 20 | 1.6× giant size, smash ground hazards, 6 s |
| Lightning | 20 | Up to 60% extra speed plus automatic dodging, 6 s |
| Green | 20 | One-hit shield, up to 8 s |
| Purple | 20 | Pull nearby fruits toward the hero, 6 s |
| Flame | 20 | Any starter can walk on magma, 6 s |
| Ice blue | 20 | Any starter can walk on water, 6 s |
| Star | 40 | Protection from hazards, capture balls, and pits, 5 s |

Timers freeze while paused/evolving. Movement/protection powers defer expiration until clear of danger, avoiding an expiration that drops the hero into a pit. Turbo eases speed up/down and owns jump control. Giant size is visual with hazard-smashing protection; the ordinary 10×14 collision body remains consistent.

Displayed score = distance / 8 + fruit points + defeated-character bonuses − crate penalties. Evolution occurs at **300 and 800 score**, waits for safe ground, and never skips the middle form. It no longer uses 30/75-second timers. The HUD shows the next threshold and active power durations; the menu includes a Fruit powers guide.

### Elemental pits

Water, magma, and rock pits have distinct surfaces and labeled warning signs. Widths are roughly 30–38 logical pixels initially, rising toward 40–48 with base speed. Width selection stays safe even if turbo expires.

- Fire starters cross magma safely.
- Water starters cross water safely.
- Grass starters cross rock pits safely.
- These advantages persist across evolutions. Incompatible pits need a jump unless a temporary power protects you. A green surface line marks pits currently safe for the hero.

The user explicitly requested fire/magma immunity and type-compatible pits generally. The complete three-type mapping above, the other five fruit effects, and the 300/800 thresholds were implementation choices stated in chat. Two optional clarification questions received no answer; the defaults were stated before proceeding. Revisit if the user wants a different mapping.

### Team Rocket

- Meowth balloon visits after about 20 seconds, marks a clear spot, and drops one ball. It is harmless while falling, opens on the ground, and becomes a stationary capture trap carried left by world scrolling.
- Touching an open ball captures the Pokémon: it closes and wobbles, then game over. Jumping clears it. The balloon no longer drops rocks.
- Jessie/James ambushes throw rolling capture balls 1.25 seconds apart, followed by the character. Jump over the character for dizzy eyes and +25 points.
