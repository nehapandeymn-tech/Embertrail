# Art provenance

- All sprite sheets in `Resources/Art/` (the ember gecko, thistle hedgehog and ripple newt families, critters and explorer, the moth-balloon gang, orbs, obstacles, signs, terrain, water, parallax biomes and the bitmap font) are original drafts made with AI image generation for this project.
- They are not ripped game sprites, and they don't come from a downloaded asset pack.
- The chunky pixel style was inspired by [Arks' Dino Characters](https://arks.itch.io/dino-characters). That pack is licensed CC BY 4.0, but none of its files are included here, and its licence doesn't apply to these sheets.
- No third-party assets are bundled, so there are no attribution lines to give.
- No licence has been chosen yet for the code or the art.
- The thistle hedgehog and ripple newt sheets were generated as JPGs on white. `Tools/keyout.swift` made their backgrounds transparent.
